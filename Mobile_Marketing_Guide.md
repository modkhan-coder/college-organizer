# Mobile Marketing Guide: Capture the Best Views

Now that your mobile app foundation is ready, you need high-quality visuals for the App Store and Google Play. Here are the "Hero Views" and instructions for recording.

## 📸 Top 5 Screenshots to Capture

1.  **The Command Center (Dashboard)**: Show the overview of GPA, Upnext assignments, and the "Flame" progress icon.
2.  **AI Study Studio**: A shot of the chat interface generating a study plan or summarizing a PDF.
3.  **The Planner**: A clean view of the calendar/list showing automated assignment scheduling.
4.  **Grade Predictor (GPA)**: The "What do I need on the Final?" calculator—this is a huge hook for students.
5.  **Social Study (Achievements)**: Show off the badges and level progress.

---

## 📽️ How to Record Clean Demos

### 1. Enable PII Masking
Before recording, go to **Profile & Settings** and use a "Demo Name" (e.g., *Student Master*) so your personal email/name isn't in the marketing videos.

### 2. Record in the iOS Simulator
1.  Open `/ios/App/App.xcworkspace` in **Xcode**.
2.  Select **iPhone 15 Pro** (or latest) as the target.
3.  Press `Cmd + R` to run.
4.  Use `Cmd + Shift + 5` to record the specific simulation window.

### 3. Record in Android Studio
1.  Open the `/android` folder in **Android Studio**.
2.  Launch a **Pixel 7** (or latest) emulator.
3.  Use the build-in Screen Record tool in the Emulator toolbar (vertical dots -> Record Screen).

---

## 💡 Pro-Tip for "Native Feel"
When recording, use the **Pull-to-Refresh** gesture on the Dashboard. It looks very polished in videos and demonstrates that the app is "Alive" and syncing data in real-time.
